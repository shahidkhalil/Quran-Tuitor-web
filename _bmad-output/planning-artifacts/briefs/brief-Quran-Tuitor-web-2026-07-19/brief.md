# Product Brief: Quran Tutor Marketplace

## Executive Summary

Quran Tutor Marketplace is a **global** managed marketplace where parents browse and book verified Quran tutors for their children, while the platform owns vetting, payments, messaging, support, and disputes. Standard currency is **USD**. It combines freedom of choice with trust-and-safety operations—unlike freelancers (choice without trust) or academies (trust narrative without choice). Tutors grow a teaching business on fair, platform-managed economics across the English-speaking Muslim diaspora.

## The Problem

English-speaking Muslim families in the diaspora (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, UAE expats, and beyond) want consistent, correct Quran learning for their children that fits school and work life. Local mosque classes are often inflexible, far away, or too large. Online options have grown, but families still face a bad tradeoff:

- **Freelance tutors** (WhatsApp, Facebook, Fiverr, referrals) offer choice and lower prices, but weak verification, last-minute cancellations, awkward disputes, and serious child-safety risk on unmonitored calls.
- **Online Quran academies** offer more structure and a trust story, but typically *assign* tutors, limit comparison/choice, and often suffer opaque billing, weak support, or teacher churn that resets a child’s progress.
- **Early Quran marketplaces** are closer to the need, but trust and operations quality remain uneven.

This is not a shortage of “another online Quran academy.” The unmet need is a **managed marketplace**: families can **browse and choose** verified tutors, compare profiles, book classes, pay securely, and get ongoing support — while the platform owns vetting, scheduling, payments, customer support, and dispute resolution. Without that combination of **choice + trust + managed operations**, parents remain stuck choosing between unsafe flexibility and inflexible institutions.

## Who This Serves

**Primary buyer (v1):** Parents in the **English-speaking Muslim diaspora** booking for children aged **4–16**.

**Primary learner:** The child (Qaida, recitation, Tajweed, Hifz pathways as level allows).

**Secondary audience (supported):** Adult learners seeking Quran, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic, or Islamic studies.

**Supply-side customer:** English-capable, vetted Quran tutors (primarily Pakistan, Egypt, and other Muslim-majority countries) who want diaspora students, flexible teaching, and reliable platform payouts.

**Geography:** Global from day one for English UI + USD pricing; demand centres include USA, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, and UAE expats.

## The Solution

Quran Tutor Marketplace is a global managed online platform that connects students and parents with verified Quran tutors, allowing them to browse, compare, and book tutors with confidence while the platform handles tutor verification, scheduling, secure payments, messaging, customer support, quality assurance, and dispute resolution, enabling tutors to grow their teaching business and students to receive a trusted, flexible learning experience.

**Launch posture:** Global product (English UI, **USD** standard currency, timezone-aware availability); ops designed for diaspora families worldwide.

**v1 “managed” non-negotiables:**
- Tutor vetting before listing
- Platform-only payments (no student↔tutor direct pay)
- Parent-visible messaging for minors (no private child↔tutor channels)
- Free rematch if tutor is not a fit
- Human customer support and dispute resolution
- Progress notes / lesson feedback for students and parents

**Explicitly deferred:** Session recording — Phase 2+, optional with consent, after privacy/storage/legal design (GDPR and applicable children’s privacy frameworks).

**Core experience loop:** Browse/compare verified tutors → free trial → recurring book → platform pay → lesson → progress note → rematch/support if needed → tutor payout.

## What Makes This Different

Not another online Quran academy that assigns a teacher and hopes for the best. Two differentiators define the product:

1. **Trust and safety operations** — Verified tutors, secure platform payments, platform-managed support, transparent reviews, and fair dispute resolution. The company takes custody of the risky parts of online Quran learning for children.
2. **Freedom of choice** — Parents and students choose their tutor instead of being assigned one. They compare qualifications, teaching style, language, availability, price, ratings, and reviews.

Together: marketplace choice with managed operations that meet or beat academy trust expectations — while tutors grow a flexible teaching business on fair, transparent economics.

## Scope

### In (MVP)

- Parent/student accounts; child profiles under parent custody
- Tutor application, credential upload, admin approve/reject before listing
- Browse, filter, compare tutors (qualifications, language, gender, availability, price, ratings/reviews)
- Free trial booking (no card required to start trial)
- Recurring / weekly lesson booking and calendar
- Platform-only payments and tutor payouts
- Parent-visible messaging for minors (no private child↔tutor channels)
- Free rematch if tutor is not a fit
- Human customer support and dispute resolution
- Progress notes / lesson feedback for parents/students
- Ratings and reviews
- Attendance tracking
- Admin tools for vetting, bookings oversight, commission/settings, support cases
- Video via integrated third-party links (e.g. Zoom/Meet) — not a custom video stack

### Out (MVP — explicit)

- Session recording (Phase 2+, optional with consent)
- AI learning reports / Tajweed AI
- Group classes at scale
- Full CMS / marketing site builder
- Learning certificates program
- Featured tutor ads / paid ranking
- Day-one multi-currency wallets / local FX (standard currency is USD)
- Native in-app video infrastructure

## Vision

Become the default trusted marketplace for Quran learning in the English-speaking Muslim diaspora — where families choose verified tutors with confidence, tutors build sustainable teaching practices, and the platform sets the standard for safety, fairness, and learning quality at global scale.
