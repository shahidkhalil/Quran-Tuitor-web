# Brief Addendum — Quran Tutor Marketplace

## Session recording (deferred)

Stakeholder decision (2026-07-19): **Not in MVP.**

v1 trust substitutes: parent-visible communication, attendance tracking, lesson progress reports, human support, free tutor rematch.

Phase 2+: optional recording with consent from tutors and parents/students, after privacy, storage, and legal review (including GDPR and applicable children’s privacy frameworks for minors).

## Product decision — geography & currency (2026-07-27)

Stakeholder override: product is **global** (not UK-first beachhead). Standard currency is **USD** for listings, filters, checkout, and ledgers.
